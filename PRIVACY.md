# Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Aer Weather does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Your data is yours alone. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking software.

### What Information Does the Application Collect?

It doesn't. Aer Weather only connects to the internet to retrieve current weather conditions and forecasts from the selected weather data provider. The only data transmitted in this process are the geographic coordinates of your selected location - nothing more, and nothing more is being collected or sent anywhere else.
